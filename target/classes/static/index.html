MEDICAL PROTOCOL

Snakebite First Aid Guidance

Evidence-based medical guidance aligned with World Health Organization (WHO) protocols.

✓

Immediate Actions (DO)

  • ✓
    Seek emergency medical care immediately

    Transport the patient immediately to the nearest hospital equipped with Anti-Snake Venom (ASV).

  • ✓
    Keep the victim calm and still

    Panic and movement increase heart rate and accelerate systemic venom spread.

  • ✓
    Immobilize the affected limb

    Use a splint or loose sling to restrict movement and maintain the limb at heart level.

  • ✓
    Remove tight jewelry and clothing

    Take off rings, watches, tight bracelets, and footwear before localized swelling begins.

✕

Dangerous Myths (DO NOT)

  • ✕
    DO NOT cut or incise the wound

    Incision damages vital tendons, nerves, and vessels and causes severe blood loss and infection.

  • ✕
    DO NOT attempt to suck out the venom

    Suction is ineffective at extracting venom and introduces mouth bacteria into compromised tissue.

  • ✕
    DO NOT apply a tight arterial tourniquet

    Tourniquets stop arterial blood flow, causing limb gangrene, amputation risk, and shock on release.

  • ✕
    DO NOT apply ice or traditional herbal pastes

    Extreme cold worsens tissue necrosis. Do not delay emergency hospital transport for unverified cures.

🚨 EMERGENCY ACTION PROTOCOL

Treat any suspected bite as an immediate medical emergency. Do not wait for symptoms to develop. Transport the patient to a hospital immediately.
1
Dial Emergency Services:

India: Call 112 (National) or 108 (Ambulance).

2
Head to Hospital with ASV:

Proceed directly to the nearest district government hospital or medical college.

3
Immobilize Affected Limb:

Splint the limb and keep the patient completely still during transit.
